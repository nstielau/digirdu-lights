# Echo Glow consumer enclosure

Current prototype: [Revision 8](revision-8/README.md), **131.8 ×36.46 ×23.8 mm**, with the Feather’s onboard USB-C, stepped end seam, 13.5 mm blind-pocket snaps, domed three-button rail, and reset pinhole. Revision 8 keeps revision 7's case geometry and retires flat buttons, separate plungers, and four-button carriers.

Saved as cloud **version 5 of Echo Glow consumer v6** in the Echo Glow project. Continue in that same Fusion document for future edits; numbered disk folders are release checkpoints.

Two **60 ×10 ×2.75 mm magnets** insert vertically from the open seam, one in each half on the same narrow side. The inward slot walls are closed. Matching curved battery saddles now support the cell from both halves. The wide TFT shoulders are 0.5 mm taller, moving the header-connected boards away from the buttons; rear supports shorten accordingly.

The onboard USB-C, Ø2 mm reset pinhole, 2.5 ×0.4 mm temporary domed button rail, prototype snaps and M2 ×16 screw closure remain. Put the two zip-tie heads on the side opposite the magnets. Print the snap coupons first and test magnet fit before final assembly.
