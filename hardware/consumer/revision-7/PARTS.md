Fasteners: Ø2.3 mm clearance, Ø4.4 ×2.2 mm cap recess, nominal 6.0 mm pilot engagement and 0.7 mm tip clearance for M2 ×16. Screw centers in assembly XY are (14.6,26.16) and (139.5,-3.3) mm. Avoid overtightening or forcing a screw into a blocked pilot.

Legacy reset plungers and four-button carriers are included in the bundle for compatibility; no reset plunger is fitted to the consumer. No striped models or Bambu projects are included.

V6 adds front battery saddles and tie-head reliefs. Put both tie heads on the non-magnet side. The wide TFT support shoulders are 0.5 mm taller; the complete board stack shifts rearward 0.5 mm and the rear supports shorten accordingly. No extra hardware is required.
